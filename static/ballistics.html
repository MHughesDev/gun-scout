Methodology

Model class

Modified point-mass trajectory model (same class as commercial 4-DOF solvers): RK4 integration of drag + gravity + optional Coriolis over the air-relative velocity, with spin effects layered as empirical corrections rather than integrated forces. Drag uses the standard G1/G7 reference-projectile tables scaled by the load's ballistic coefficient (form-factor method); a marginal-stability BC penalty applies below Sg 1.5.

Muzzle velocity tiers

Measured (your chronograph, σ = SD/√n) → Trued (back-solved from observed drops) → Estimated (factory velocity rescaled to your barrel length via a Le Duc curve v(L)=aL/(L+b) fit to published chop-test data; σ = √(30² + fit rms²)) → Nominal (factory number as-is, σ = 75 fps). Powder-temperature sensitivity and revolver cylinder-gap loss are applied as class priors. Shot-to-shot SD comes from the load's class (match 12 / defensive 20 / bulk 30 fps).

Spin & stability

Gyroscopic stability via the Miller twist rule with velocity and air-density corrections; Sg < 1.0 = predicted tumble (trajectory suppressed), 1.0–1.5 = marginal (≈3% BC loss per 0.1 Sg). Spin drift 1.25·(Sg+1.2)·t^1.83 and crosswind aerodynamic jump (0.01·Sg − 0.0024·l + 0.032) MOA/mph are Litz's empirical fits, valid for spin-stabilized rifle bullets (Sg clamped to ≤2.5; suppressed for handgun/rimfire/shotgun classes where the fits are out of domain).

Uncertainty

400 Monte Carlo draws per loadout sample MV (shot SD + systematic σ), BC tolerance (2–3.5%), wind gusting (default σ = 35% of speed) and temperature. Bands are P10–P90. Hit probability combines the Monte Carlo dispersion with your rifle's precision (an X-MOA group is treated as 3σ radial, so σ = X/3 per axis) over a circular vital zone, via the exact 1-D reduction of the disk integral. A quoted "90% effective range" is the farthest range with P(hit) ≥ 0.90.

Known limits

Factory ammunition's powder is proprietary — no interior-ballistics simulation is attempted; measurement (chronograph or truing) is the only route to tier-1 accuracy. Fidelity degrades through the transonic band (marked on charts). Miller's rule reads conservative for plastic-tipped and monolithic bullets. Barrel-curve scaling is an estimate: ±30–50 fps is normal gun-to-gun variation even at the same barrel length.
